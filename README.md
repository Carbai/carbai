### 📚 Data books I'd recommend

- **Weapons of Math Destruction** — Cathy O'Neil.
  How opaque algorithms encode bias and cause real harm at scale. Essential reading on the ethics of modelling.
  Easy to read, it is the one i'd usually recommend to people without technical experience. It would upset you though.
- **Invisible Women** — Caroline Criado Perez.
  How missing data erases women from decisions built on it. A powerful case study in what gaps in data actually cost.
  One of my favourites!! Real cases where products and policies were built and even called successes while quietly harming or ignoring women, because the data never accounted for them.
- **Calling Bullshit** — Carl T. Bergstrom & Jevin D. West.
  A practical field guide to spotting misleading data, charts, and statistical claims.
  This is more technical but the proof that statistics can be fun.
- **Naked Statistics** — Charles Wheelan.
  Statistical intuition made genuinely readable, the "why," not just the formulas.
- **NOW READING - Empire of AI** - Karen Hao
